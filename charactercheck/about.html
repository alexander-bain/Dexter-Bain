OPEN BOOK How Character Check is made

About the sources.

Character Check is a fan-made personality game with thousands of screened quizzes. Every public quiz must have sourced promotional artwork and four named results. This page explains where the title information, images, and question ideas come from.

01

Photos and title data

Show listings, cast information, episode images, and links use the TVmaze API. TVmaze describes its API data license on its licensing page.

Film research and linked imagery use Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, and source links to IMDb. Image rights stay with their respective owners. Every quiz image includes a visible source link beside it.

02

The screened catalog

The large catalog collections are assembled from Wikidata using the Wikidata Query Service. Titles are deduplicated, then screened before publishing. The research pool contains 10,000 titles, but an entry appears in the public quiz wall only after it has a sourced image and four real named character or participant outcomes.

“Most famous” is measured by the number of Wikipedia-language sites linking to a Wikidata title, after titles already present in Character Check are removed. That makes the documentary list reproducible, but it is a popularity proxy—not an official all-time ranking.

03

Where the question ideas come from

The question wording and answer choices are original Character Check writing. The first 156 quizzes have hand-authored prompts based on their recognizable settings and premises. Every other title receives ten deterministic, title-specific scenarios shaped by its format, collection, and public genre metadata from TVmaze, Wikidata, and Wikipedia.

Questions are not copied from scripts, reviews, personality tests, or fan sites. A full-catalog test checks that all ten prompts are present, distinct within each quiz, and identifiable to that title.

04

Artwork quality rule

Generated title cards may be used while catalog entries are being researched, but they are not published as finished quiz artwork. A public quiz card must use a sourced promotional image with its source linked from the quiz.

Entries without a sourced image or four named matches stay hidden until both pieces are verified. This keeps unfinished catalog records out of the playable experience.

05

Requested titles checked directly

The documentary collection includes You Can See Everything. The reality collection includes Nathan for You and The Rehearsal.
