About the project

One snowglobe.
A lot of television.

The Dream Tree is an interactive map of the Tommy Westphall Universe theory. It follows television crossovers, spin-offs, and recurring fictional details until every displayed route reaches St. Elsewhere.

How the map works

A show's degree is the number of visible show-to-show links in its shortest recorded route to St. Elsewhere. The map preserves its original 452-show routes and adds only series for which a usable connection could be traced back to that graph. A film, television special, character, franchise, or fictional brand may bridge two shows, but only television shows are drawn as nodes.

Included
  • Character crossovers and shared episodes
  • Official spin-offs, sequels, and continuations
  • Recurring fictional brands, places, companies, or props
  • Documented cross-media bridges whose complete route is named
  • Routes that can be explained link by link
Not counted
  • Actors appearing as unrelated characters
  • Real-world brands or ordinary historical references
  • Links labeled disputed, non-contiguous, or unsubstantiated
  • Dream, fantasy, parody, or behind-the-scenes appearances alone
